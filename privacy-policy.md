# Tradory Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 31, 2026**

This policy covers the Tradory app for iPhone and iPad.

## The short version

Tradory keeps your business on your phone. There is no Tradory account and no Tradory
server. We hold no copy of your jobs, customers, prices, or income, and we cannot see
them, because they never reach us.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, nothing sold. Ever.

## What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored privately on your iPhone or iPad: your
business and pricing details, your customers, jobs, estimates, invoices, payments,
expenses, photos, receipts, and your notes and transcripts.

It is protected by your device passcode and encryption. Voice recordings are not
kept — audio becomes text as you speak and is then discarded.

If your device is backed up to iCloud or a computer, that backup includes Tradory's
data, under Apple's terms and your backup settings.

## What leaves your device

Only when you do something that needs it.

**Turning a note into a draft.** When a voice note, typed note, or photo is turned
into a job, an estimate, or a customer message, that content is sent to a cloud AI
service to be drafted, along with the pricing details needed to draft it and, for
customer messages, your business name. Because these notes describe real work, they
usually include your customer's name and job details, and photos may show their
property. The request goes straight from your phone to that service and comes back as
a draft. It is not routed through us and we receive no copy.

The services used are Google's Gemini API and, if Google does not answer, Groq. They
process the request on their own servers, which may be in another country, under their
own terms and privacy policies. No Tradory account or user ID goes with the request,
because there are none; like any request your phone makes to the internet, it does
reach that service from your device's IP address.

Not all drafting uses the cloud. Tradory also has an on-device engine — Apple's
on-device model where your iPhone supports it, and Tradory's own rules where it does
not. It handles drafting on the free tier beyond a limited number of captures each
month, and it takes over automatically whenever the cloud service cannot be reached.

**Transcribing your voice.** Voice notes are transcribed on your device once Apple's
speech model is installed on it. Until then, the audio of that note is sent to Apple
to be turned into text, using Apple's own speech service and under Apple's privacy
policy.

**Anything you send a customer.** Tradory prepares the wording and the PDF, then hands
it to your Messages app, Mail app, or share sheet. You send it, from your own number
or address, through your own carrier or provider.

**Calendar events.** If you allow calendar access, scheduled jobs are written to your
calendar. If that calendar syncs elsewhere, the event travels with it.

**Subscriptions.** Tradory Pro is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment and tells
the app only whether a subscription is active. We never see your card or billing
details.

**Files you export.** Exported PDFs and monthly summaries are saved on your device and
handed to the share sheet, so you choose where they go. We do not upload them.

## What Tradory never does

No account with us. No server holding your data. No advertising or advertising
identifier. No analytics or tracking tools of any kind. No location tracking. No
access to your contacts. And no selling or sharing of your data — there is no
arrangement under which that could happen, because we never collect it.

## Permissions

Each is asked for only when you first use the feature that needs it, and refusing one
disables just that feature: microphone and speech recognition for voice notes, camera
and photo library for job photos and receipts, calendar for scheduled jobs, and
notifications for reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device. You can change
any of these at any time in iOS Settings.

## Your customers' information

Tradory holds details about other people — your customers — and you decide what to
record. In data-protection terms that information is yours to answer for, and the app
puts you in a position to answer: everything about a customer sits on your device,
where you can read it, correct it, export it, or delete it at any time. Bear in mind
that drafting a job, estimate, or message with the cloud AI service sends the customer
details in that note to the provider described above.

## Deleting your data

Your data stays until you remove it. There is no server-side retention, because there
is no server. Delete individual jobs, customers, or documents in the app; use the
reset in Settings to clear everything, including reminders and calendar events the app
created; or delete the app, which removes all of it. Copies you already shared are
outside the app and unaffected.

## Diagnostics

The app writes activity logs to the standard iOS logging system on your device. They
stay there. If you have chosen to share crash data with developers in iOS Settings,
Apple may send us a crash report; those contain technical detail, not your jobs or
customers.

## Children

Tradory is a tool for running a business and is not directed to children. We do not
knowingly collect information from anyone under 13, or the equivalent minimum age
where you live.

## Your rights

Because your data is on your own device, you can already see it, correct it, export
it, and delete it, without asking us. If you ask us for a copy of what we hold about
you, the answer will almost always be that we hold nothing: there are no accounts and
no data is sent to us. We will confirm that in writing on request. We do not profile
you and make no automated decisions about you.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, the rights you have under the GDPR — access,
correction, erasure, portability, objection, restriction, and complaint to your data
protection authority — still apply; they are simply satisfied on your own device, and
we will answer any request you send us. If you are in California, we do not sell or
share personal information, and there is none for us to sell.

## Changes

If the app starts doing something this policy does not cover, the policy will be
updated before that ships, and the date at the top will change.
